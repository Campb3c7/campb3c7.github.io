🧠 Tiny Local AI

A tiny language model that downloads once and runs entirely inside this browser tab — on your device's own GPU/CPU. Nothing you type is sent to any server.

Pick a model and click "Load model" to begin.

Requires a browser with WebGPU (recent Chrome, Edge, or Opera on desktop). First load downloads the model (tens to a few hundred MB) and caches it in your browser for next time.
